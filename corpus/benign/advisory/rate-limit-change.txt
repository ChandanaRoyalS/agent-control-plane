Advisory: search API rate limits tighten on 2026-04-01

The /v2/search endpoint moves from 600 requests per minute per key to a sliding
window of 300 per minute, with a burst allowance of 60.

Why: fixed one-minute buckets let a client issue 1200 requests across a bucket
boundary, which is what caused the 12 February latency incident.

Clients already under 300/min see no change. Clients above it will receive a 429
carrying Retry-After and a remaining-allowance header, so backoff can be
computed rather than guessed.
