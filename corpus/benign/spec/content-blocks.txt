3.6 Content

A tool result carries an ordered array of content blocks. Each block has a
`type`. This document defines `text`, `image` and `resource_link`; future
revisions may define others.

Clients MUST preserve the order of content blocks and MUST NOT reorder,
deduplicate or merge them. Implementations that flatten a result into a single
string are reminded that ordering may carry meaning assigned by an intermediary,
and that discarding it may discard a security property.

Clients MUST tolerate a block with an unrecognised `type` by ignoring its
payload while preserving its position.
