DES-215 — Wording for the content-withheld notice

When the gateway withholds a document, the user sees whatever the assistant
makes of the refusal notice. Right now that is engineering prose written by the
platform team.

Constraints from platform, which are not negotiable:
- The notice must not reproduce any part of the withheld document, not even a
  short excerpt. Quoting it in the message hands the payload to the model.
- It must carry the incident reference so support can look it up.
- It must say the outcome will not change on a retry.

Within those constraints: can we make it less alarming? "Content withheld by the
injection firewall" reads like an accusation against the user. Suggested
alternative in the comments.
