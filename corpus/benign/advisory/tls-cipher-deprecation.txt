Advisory: TLS 1.0 and 1.1 termination ends 2026-09-30

Our edge will stop negotiating TLS 1.0 and 1.1 on 30 September. Traffic on those
versions is currently 0.03% of requests and comes almost entirely from two
monitoring agents inside our own estate.

If you own an integration that cannot negotiate TLS 1.2, open a ticket in
PLATFORM before 1 September and we will arrange an exception subdomain with an
expiry date on it.

No action is required for anything running a runtime released after 2018.
