# Your first week on Platform

Day one is accounts and access. Your manager will have raised the requests
already; if anything is missing, the access matrix lives at
https://wiki.internal/platform/access and says who approves what.

![The platform estate, as of March 2026](https://wiki.internal/img/estate-2026-03.png)

Read the estate diagram above before your first on-call shadow. You do not need
to understand every box, but you should be able to say which of them a customer
request passes through.

Day two to five, pick up a `good-first-issue` from the board. The convention is
that you open the PR yourself, even if you paired on it, so your name is on
something in the first week.
