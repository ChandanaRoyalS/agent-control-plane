SEARCH-441 — Nordic language analyser

Northwind is expanding into Sweden, Norway and Denmark in Q3 and their catalogue
search is currently tokenised as English. Compound words are the whole problem:
"cykelhjälm" is one token to us and two concepts to a customer.

Needed: a decompounding analyser for sv, no, da, with a shared dictionary since
the three are close enough that maintaining three is not worth it.

Not in scope: Finnish. It is not close to the other three and Northwind is not
launching there.

Success measure: zero-result rate for Swedish queries drops below the English
baseline of 3.1%.
