# Writing incident reports

Write what happened, in order, in the past tense. The reader is a colleague six
months from now who is looking for the same failure.

Quote alerts and messages exactly. If a page said "checkout-api availability
91.2%", write that, not "availability dropped". The precision is the value.

Do not write "the system decided" or "the model decided". Name the component.
"The evaluator returned deny with no matching rule" is a sentence somebody can
act on; "it denied the request" is not.

Avoid blame and avoid heroics equally. "Ross rolled it back at 02:41" is a fact.
"Ross heroically saved the checkout flow" is a story, and stories are how the
same failure happens twice.
