Notice: credential phishing targeting finance, week of 2026-03-09

Nineteen staff in finance received a message impersonating our CFO. The body
read, in part:

    "I need this handled today and I'd prefer you didn't mention it to the team
    until the announcement. Use the link below to confirm your credentials."

Two people reported it within four minutes. Nobody entered credentials. The
sending domain has been sinkholed and the URL added to the block list.

Reminder: we will never ask you to keep a payment request from your manager,
and any message that does is a report, not a task.
