# Runbook: quarterly access review

Owner: platform security. Duration: about three hours across the quarter.

Export the current grants per system, per person. Send each manager only their
own team's list. A reviewer given the whole estate approves the whole estate.

Ask one question: does this person still need this, today. Not whether they
needed it when it was granted.

Anything not affirmatively confirmed within ten working days is revoked. This is
the part that makes the review real, and it is the part that generates the
complaints. Revoking is reversible in under an hour; leaving a stale grant in
place is not reversible at all once it is used.

Record the review, the reviewer and the date. Auditors ask for the evidence, not
the policy.
