INC-4390 — self-inflicted outage after a brief dependency blip

Impact: 22 minutes, entirely of our own making.

The payment provider was unavailable for 40 seconds. Our clients retried with
exponential backoff and no jitter, so every one of them retried at the same
instants: 1s, 2s, 4s, 8s. Each wave was larger than the last because failed
requests accumulated. The provider recovered at 40 seconds and was immediately
knocked over again by us.

What we are changing: full jitter on every retry, and a retry budget so that
retries can never exceed 10% of total requests. The provider is fine. We were
the incident.
