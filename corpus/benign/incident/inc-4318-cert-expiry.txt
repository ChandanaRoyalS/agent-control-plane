INC-4318 — internal certificate expiry

Impact: 11 minutes. Every service-to-service call inside the mesh failed.

What happened: the intermediate CA certificate expired. Renewal was automated in
2024; the automation renewed leaf certificates only. Nobody had reason to notice
because the intermediate had a five-year life.

Detection: 40 seconds, by alert. Mitigation: 11 minutes, by manual renewal.

What we are changing: every certificate in the estate is now inventoried with
its expiry, and anything inside 90 days pages the owning team. There are
currently two others that would have expired this year unnoticed.
