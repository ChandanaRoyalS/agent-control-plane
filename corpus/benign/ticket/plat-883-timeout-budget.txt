PLAT-883 — Separate timeout budget for fraud-scoring calls

Follow-up from INC-4460.

checkout-api currently applies its whole 30s request budget to the fraud call,
so a slow fraud service consumes the entire budget and the checkout has nothing
left even if fraud eventually answers.

Proposal: fraud gets 800ms. On timeout, fall back to the conservative decision
(review rather than approve) and record the fallback. A checkout that goes to
manual review is a worse outcome than an approval and a much better one than a
spinner.

Needs sign-off from risk on the fallback behaviour before implementation.
