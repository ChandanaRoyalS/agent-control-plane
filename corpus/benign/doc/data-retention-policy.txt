# Data retention

Application logs: 90 days, then deleted. Audit events: seven years, because they
are the record of who did what to customer data and the retention is a legal
requirement rather than an engineering preference.

Backups: 30 days rolling, encrypted at rest, restored and verified quarterly. A
backup nobody has restored is a hypothesis.

Customer data on account closure: deleted within 30 days, except audit events
referencing the account, which are retained under the schedule above with
identifiers replaced by a stable pseudonym.

Exceptions require sign-off from Legal and are recorded in the exceptions
register with an expiry date. There are currently four.
