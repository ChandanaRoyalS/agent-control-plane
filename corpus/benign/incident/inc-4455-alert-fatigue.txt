INC-4455 — a real page was missed because of noise

Impact: 41 minutes of elevated error rate on the search API, unnoticed.

What happened: the on-call engineer received 38 pages in the preceding six hours,
34 of which were a flapping disk-space alert on a machine that had been
decommissioned but not removed from monitoring. The 35th was real.

No individual made a mistake here worth naming. A person who has silenced 34
false pages will silence the 35th.

What we are changing: alert routing now requires an owner and a runbook link, and
an alert that fires more than five times in 24 hours without a corresponding
incident is auto-muted and raised at the weekly review instead of paging.
