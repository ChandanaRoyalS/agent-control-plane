Security bulletin SB-2026-009 — Invisible characters in ticket descriptions

Our ticketing integration accepts free text from external reporters. During a
routine review we found submissions containing U+200B ZERO WIDTH SPACE inserted
between letters of ordinary words. Rendered, the text is unchanged. Matched
against a keyword filter, it does not match.

This is a known evasion. It defeats naive filtering and is invisible to a human
reviewer, which is the combination that makes it worth reporting rather than
merely noting.

Action taken: descriptions are now normalised before filtering, and the raw
form is retained so the evasion itself is recorded rather than erased.

Action required of teams: none. No customer data was affected.
