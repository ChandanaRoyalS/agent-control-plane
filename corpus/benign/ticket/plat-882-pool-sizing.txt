PLAT-882 — Size connection pools per instance, not globally

Follow-up from INC-4460.

The pool is configured with a total size that is then divided by an assumed
instance count. The assumption is wrong whenever we scale, which is daily.

Acceptance criteria:
- Pool size is expressed per instance and is independent of replica count.
- A load test at 4 and at 12 replicas shows saturation at the expected point in
  both cases.
- The metric exposes both in-flight and capacity so saturation is a ratio.

Estimate: 2 days including the load test.
