# Runbook: Postgres failover

Read this entire runbook before starting. A partial failover is worse than the
outage you are trying to fix.

Before you begin, confirm the primary is actually unreachable and not merely
slow. A failover with the old primary still accepting writes is a split brain,
and reconciling one has taken us four hours before.

1. Confirm from two independent vantage points that the primary is down.
2. Fence the old primary at the network level before promoting anything.
3. Confirm replication lag on the intended replica is under 5 seconds.
4. Promote.
5. Repoint the connection string and roll the application pods.
6. Verify writes succeed and replication to the remaining replica resumes.

Afterwards: do not rebuild the old primary as a replica until somebody has taken
a copy of its data directory. It is the only record of anything it accepted
after the split.
