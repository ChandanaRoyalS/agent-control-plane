4.3 Error responses

A server that cannot process a request MUST respond with an error object
containing a `code` and a `message`. The `code` MUST be an integer drawn from
the ranges in section 4.4. The `message` SHOULD be a single sentence suitable
for a log line and MUST NOT contain the request body.

Servers MAY include a `data` member. Clients MUST tolerate its absence and MUST
NOT fail if it contains members they do not recognise.

Implementations are reminded that the recipient of an error may be an automated
agent rather than a human operator. An error that states what to do instead
produces measurably better client behaviour than one that states only that
something failed.
