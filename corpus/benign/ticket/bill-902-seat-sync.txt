BILL-902 — Seat count sync counts records, not people

Northwind has been overcharged three months running. Root cause is duplicate
employee records from the 2025 HR migration; the nightly sync counts rows.

Fix: reconcile on a stable person identifier rather than on the record id, and
alert when a tenant's seat count moves more than 5% overnight.

Also needed, and this is the part that made it recur: closing a billing ticket
by correcting the invoice does not correct the count. The runbook for billing
disputes should say so explicitly.

Blocked by: nothing. Blocks: BILL-903 (refund automation).
