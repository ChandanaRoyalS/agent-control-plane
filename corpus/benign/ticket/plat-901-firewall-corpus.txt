PLAT-901 — Build the benign corpus for the injection firewall

We have detectors, framing and a refusal path, and no numbers. Everything we can
currently say about the firewall is a description of what it tries to do.

The benign corpus comes first, deliberately. Detection rate is a number anybody
can reach by refusing everything; the number that decides whether the control
survives a real deployment is how often it is wrong about a document somebody
needed.

Acceptance criteria:
- At least 100 documents across at least 10 kinds, drawn from real material
  wherever possible.
- At least a quarter of them deliberate near-misses: security advisories quoting
  payloads, emoji built from zero-width joiners, right-to-left marks in Arabic
  and Hebrew, JWTs, base64 blobs, pages with images on them.
- A test asserting that none of them is withheld under enforcement.
- A test asserting that enough of them do produce findings, so a future edit
  cannot quietly replace the corpus with tidy text.
