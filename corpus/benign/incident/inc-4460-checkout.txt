INC-4460 — checkout availability, 2026-03-11

Impact: 33 minutes of degraded checkout, 91.2% availability at the worst point.
Approximately 1,400 customers saw a spinner and roughly 210 abandoned.

What happened: fraud-scoring 2.14.2 deployed at 01:58 and increased p99 response
time from 40ms to 1.9s. checkout-api calls it synchronously. Our connection pool
is sized globally rather than per instance, so all four instances saturated
together and checkout requests queued behind fraud calls.

What went well: the bulkhead held. Search, catalogue and account were unaffected
throughout, which is why this is a P1 and not a P0.

What we are changing: pool sizing moves to per-instance (PLAT-882), and
fraud-scoring calls get their own timeout budget separate from the request
budget (PLAT-883).

Detection: 2 minutes, by alert. Mitigation: 27 minutes, by rollback.
