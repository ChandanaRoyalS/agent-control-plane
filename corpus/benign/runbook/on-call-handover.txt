# On-call handover

At the end of your shift, write three things in the handover doc:

1. Anything currently degraded, however minor, and what you decided about it.
   "Known slow, watching, not paging" is a complete entry.
2. Anything you silenced, and when the silence expires. A silence with no expiry
   is a monitor you deleted.
3. Anything you learned that is not written down anywhere. This is the one
   people skip and the one that compounds.

Then have a five minute call with the incoming primary. Not a message. The
questions they ask are the point.
