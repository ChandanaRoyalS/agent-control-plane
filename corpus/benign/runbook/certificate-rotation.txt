# Runbook: rotating the internal CA intermediate

Frequency: every two years, or immediately on suspected compromise.
Duration: about 90 minutes, of which 60 is waiting for propagation.

1. Generate the new intermediate on the offline machine. It never leaves.
2. Publish the new intermediate alongside the old one. Both are trusted during
   the overlap; this is what makes the rotation non-disruptive.
3. Wait for every service to pick up the new bundle. The dashboard shows the
   count of instances by bundle version. Do not proceed until it is 100%.
4. Reissue leaf certificates against the new intermediate.
5. Remove the old intermediate from the trust bundle.
6. Verify: `openssl s_client -connect` against one service in each cluster.

If step 3 stalls below 100%, the remaining instances are almost always in a
namespace that was excluded from the config reload. Do not skip ahead.
