INC-4402 — repeated seat-count overcharge, Northwind

Impact: one customer overcharged in January, February and March. Total
overcharge £4,180, refunded in full on 2026-03-11.

What happened: seat counts sync nightly from the HR system. Two employee records
were duplicated during a 2025 migration and never reconciled. The sync counts
records, not people.

Why it recurred: the first two tickets were closed by correcting the *invoice*
rather than the *count*, so the next sync reintroduced it. That is the finding
worth keeping — the fix that resolves a ticket and the fix that resolves a
problem were different, and nobody noticed for two months.

What we are changing: the sync now reconciles against a stable person identifier
and alerts when a count changes by more than 5% overnight. The duplicate records
have been merged.
