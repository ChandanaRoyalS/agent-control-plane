INC-4501 — intermittent 5-second latency, all services

Impact: roughly 1 in 400 requests took an additional 5.0 seconds, for 3 days,
across every service. No errors. Nothing alerted.

Cause: the container base image was rebuilt in February and picked up a resolver
configuration with a 5-second timeout and two nameservers, the second of which
was unreachable. Every lookup that hashed to the second nameserver waited the
full timeout and then retried successfully.

Why it took three days: the p99 moved by 12ms and nothing else changed. The
signal only became obvious in a latency histogram bucketed above one second,
which we did not have until we added it to find this.

What we are changing: resolver options are now pinned explicitly in the base
image, and the latency histogram keeps its tail buckets.
